Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: flagship mascot concept sheet for a contemporary Korean subway departure-coach website.
Primary request: An ORIGINAL anthropomorphic SUBWAY TRAIN character. The user rejected an earlier animal mascot as old-fashioned, pathetic and weak. This new design must feel STRONG, confident, bold, effortlessly adorable and stylish. It is a train, never an animal. Tsundere: blunt tough talk but protective care for its passenger.
Subject design: a short squat wide subway front carriage, sturdy rounded rectangular body, low center of gravity, large chunky soles planted firmly, short thick arms with simple mitten fists. The body is the actual train carriage, NOT a person wearing a train helmet. Recognizable train details reduced to a few decisive graphic features: large dark windshield containing two bold expressive eyes, a pair of small square headlights, one simple side sliding door and wheels visible under the chassis. Broad powerful shape, no skinny limbs. Front three-quarter view shows train depth. Lime green main body, near-black windshield and outlines, ivory eye shapes, one small violet accent. Dark confident eyebrows slightly lowered, alert eyes, tiny asymmetric confident smirk; cute assertive little bouncer energy. Compact friendly proportions, no sharp teeth, no aggressive weaponry. Strong and lovable at once, emotionally assured, not distressed or pleading. No blush, no sweat drops, no floppy ears, no fur, no tail.
Style: contemporary independent designer character, exceptionally clean flat 2D graphic mascot, thick intentional black contours, a few bold solid color blocks, minimal hard shadow if needed. Distinctive silhouette legible at tiny sizes. Strong graphic wit and spare handmade personality. NO beige stationery aesthetic, NO watercolor, NO soft airbrush, NO gradients, NO shiny 3D toy render, NO elaborate robot armor, NO generic emoji, NO ornamental sparkle.
Composition: square presentation sheet on plain very pale cool gray, generous blank space. One large hero character on the left taking about two thirds of the composition: firmly planted feet, one fist resting at its hip, other hand making a confident thumb gesture toward its open passenger door, expression says 'Get in. I have you.' On the right two smaller poses of the EXACT same train character, same colors, same shape, same face and limbs: top, leaning forward to urge the passenger along with a determined pointing gesture; bottom, stubbornly looking away while one strong arm holds its own sliding passenger door open for someone, subtly caring. Expressive but not frantic. No extra characters. NO text, no lettering, no speech bubbles, no numbers, no labels, no logos, no watermark. Make it striking, strong, contemporary, adorable and memorable, not a generic cute train illustration.
