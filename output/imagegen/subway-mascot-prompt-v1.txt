Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: original flagship mascot concept sheet for a Korean subway departure-coach website.
Create an irresistibly cute ORIGINAL flying squirrel mascot with a tsundere personality: gruff scolding exterior, deeply caring protective actions. Momonga means the round soft flying-squirrel animal appeal here; do not reproduce any existing manga or franchise character.
Design: a squishy little pear/dumpling-shaped creamy ivory flying squirrel, tiny rounded ears, very short feet, broad cheeks, small dark espresso oval eyes with one simple white glint each, tiny inverted-v mouth, subtle dusty-pink cheek marks. Two tiny uneven eyebrow strokes convey a pout without looking cruel. Slightly messy forehead tuft with just two tips. A large soft tail curls around its side like a protective blanket. Small cape-like gliding membranes visibly join the arms to sides, an identifiable flying squirrel silhouette. One modest lime-green crossbody transit-card pouch, simple violet strap; no outfit clutter. Its own clear memorable silhouette, readable at avatar size.
Style: a professional Korean independent illustrator's charming character stationery design, flat solid colors, deliberate slightly irregular thick dark-brown ink contours, subtle natural asymmetry, economical shapes, restrained human-drawn charm. Almost no shading. Not a glossy 3D plush, not anime rendering, not AI fantasy art. No fur detail, no gradients, no dramatic lighting, no sparkle decoration, no excessive glossy eyes.
Composition: a polished square character sheet on warm off-white paper, generous breathing room. One large full-body hero pose occupying the left half: head turned away in a stubborn pout and one little paw beckoning the viewer to hurry, while the tail curls protectively toward viewer. On right, three smaller distinct full-body expressive poses arranged vertically, all exactly the same character proportions and accessories: top, urgently running and looking back worriedly to check on you; middle, pretending to be indifferent while offering its fluffy tail as a warm cover; bottom, relieved bashful tiny smile with ears slightly lowered after you catch the train. The emotional contrast must be conveyed through acting and simple facial marks. Cute first, grumpy second, sincere warmth underneath. No human hands, no additional characters, no speech bubbles, no text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. The outcome should feel like a real coherent mascot ready to develop into stickers.
